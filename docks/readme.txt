Codename Cygnus for Windows

Codename Cygnus is an interactive radio drama, first made by Reactive Studios for the iPhone and iPad.
You are a secret agent of the Cygnus agency, and each episode is a fully voiced spy story.
At every turn you choose what your agent does, such as charismatic or hostile, bold or secretive.
Your choices shape your agent's personality and change how the story goes.
The original is no longer on the App Store, and this is a port of it for Windows, played with the keyboard and your screen reader.
In the original you spoke your choices aloud, and the port lets you do that too, with a setting to turn it on or off.

All of Mission 1, its eight episodes, can be played from start to end.

Installing

Extract the zip; it gives you a folder called CodenameCygnus-Windows.
Run CodenameCygnus.exe in that folder.
The first time you open an episode, choose Download Mission 1 to fetch its recordings.
To update the game, extract the new version in place of the old folder.
Your progress, settings, achievements and the downloaded recordings are kept in the CodenameCygnus folder in your AppData folder, never in the game's own folder, so an update keeps them all.

Headphones

Headphones are recommended.
The original says so itself: "The acoustic experience of Codename Cygnus is best with the use of a high quality headset."

Starting the game

The game says its name and a loading message, and the first time, the headphones notice.
Then comes the main menu.
Continue, while an episode is in progress, takes you back to where you left off.
Episodes lists the eight episodes.
Profile says your dominant trait, how far you have come, your four trait scales and your achievements.
Settings has the volumes, speech recognition and the key bindings.
Credits lists the team who made Codename Cygnus.
Quit, or Escape, closes the game.

In every menu, Up and Down move, as do Left and Right, Home and End go to the first and last row, Enter chooses, and Escape goes back.
Back is always the first row.
Moving past either end of a menu wraps round to the other.

The episodes

Mission 1 has eight episodes.
Prologue: extract yourself from the Krasnoff Mission.
Art Show: gain information on Neptune and the hostages.
Neptune's Lair: infiltrate the Waterfront Towers to eavesdrop on Neptune.
Consulate: stop TRIDENT to save the hostages.
Lazarus Awakens: discover the origin of Lazarus.
Neptune's Gift: learn more about Neptune's motivations.
Signum Cygni: escape a perilous situation as a former threat resurfaces.
Holiday Party: fondly recall last year's holiday party with Cygnus.
A new game starts with the Prologue and Holiday Party open.
Each of the other episodes opens when you finish the one before it.
Holiday Party has timed choices: if you do not choose within seven seconds, the story moves on without you.

Episodes says whether each episode is in progress, completed or locked.
Enter on an episode opens its own screen.
An episode you have not started has its description and Start.
One in progress has its description, Continue, and Restart episode.
One you have finished has what was said at its end, Replay episode, and Preview next episode, which plays a short teaser of the episode after it.
A locked one has its description and Start, which tells you why it cannot be played yet.
Restarting or replaying an episode asks first, with the original's warning: everything saved after that episode is erased, and the episodes after it are locked again.

Downloading Mission 1

The game's download is small, and Mission 1's recordings come separately, about 469 megabytes, as the original's episodes did.
Until they are downloaded, each episode's screen has "Download Mission 1" in place of its buttons; Continue in the main menu downloads them first.
The game plays a short sound and says how far it has got every 10 percent, and Enter says it at any time; Escape cancels.
While you are in another window, it only plays the sound, so the download never talks over what you are doing.
Once the download is whole, the game checks it, fetches it again if it arrived damaged, and then the episodes can be played.
The recordings are kept in the missions folder of the game's save folder, so updating the game never downloads them again.

Playing an episode

Each part of the story plays by itself, and when a question is asked, the choices come.
The first choice is read, and Up and Down, or Left and Right, move through the others.
Home and End go to the first and last choice.
Enter chooses the one you are on, and the number keys 1 to 9 choose by number.
C says every choice again.

While the story plays:
R plays the current part again from its start.
Left and Right skip 5 seconds back or forward, and holding them keeps skipping.
S skips straight on to the next choice, but only across parts you have heard to the end before; on a part you have not, it says "Not heard yet".
H says where you are, the scene heading.

At any time:
V turns speech recognition on or off, except while it is listening, as in the original; then V only says "Listening."
Shift with Home and Shift with End turn the master volume up and down.
Page Up and Page Down turn the background ambience up and down.
Shift with Page Up and Shift with Page Down turn the decision loop up and down, the sound that plays while a choice waits.
Holding a volume key keeps turning it.
Escape or P pauses the story and opens the pause menu: Resume, Restart episode, Settings and Return to menu.
Switching away from the game's window pauses it too, and it carries on when you come back.

When an episode ends, you hear "Episode complete!" and any achievements you earned.
Then a menu has what the episode says at its end, the next episode, a preview of it, and the episode's credits.
Escape or P during a preview pauses it, with Resume, Restart and Return to menu.

Speaking your choices

With speech recognition on, you can say a choice aloud instead of pressing a key, as in the original.
It starts off; turn it on with V during an episode, or in Settings.
When a choice comes, the first choice is read, then you hear "Listening."
Say one of the choices, such as "Charismatic", and it is chosen, just as Enter would choose it.
While it listens, a choice can only be spoken: Enter and the number keys say "Speak your choice.", though the arrows still read the choices.
The game listens for 15 seconds, then says "No longer listening."; then the keys choose again, and C reads the choices and listens again.
In Holiday Party's timed choices, it listens for the whole seven seconds, so with speech recognition on they can only be spoken, and if your word is not caught in time, the story moves on without you.
If it hears something that is not a choice, it says "Sorry, I did not catch that", and goes on listening.
It listens only for the words of the choice in front of you, only while that choice waits, and nothing it hears is recorded or leaves your computer.
Headphones keep the story and your screen reader out of the microphone.
If there is no microphone, the game says "No microphone found. Choose with the keys."
If Windows' privacy settings keep apps from the microphone, it says "The microphone is blocked in Windows privacy settings."; you can allow it in Windows Settings, under Privacy and security, Microphone, by turning on access for desktop apps.

Saving

The game saves your progress by itself, at every new part of the story, so you never need to save.
Continue takes you back to the start of the part you were in.
Each episode carries on the choices and traits of the one before it, as the original did, so the last episodes remember what you did in the first ones.
Your progress, the parts you have heard and your achievements are kept in the CodenameCygnus folder in your AppData folder.

Achievements

The game has 16 achievements, kept on your computer in place of the original's online ones.
Each is announced the first time you earn it, when the next choice comes or at the end of the episode, never over the story.
The Profile lists all 16, each earned or not, with what earns it.

Your agent

The Profile in the main menu shows the agent your choices have made.
Each of four traits leans one way or the other: Openness from secretive to bold, Predictability from impulsive to focused, Diplomacy from hostile to charismatic, and Activeness from clever to athletic.
A choice word at one end moves its trait that way, and your dominant trait is the one you lean furthest.
The profile also says the furthest episode you have reached, how many you have completed, and your achievements.

Settings

Each volume has its levels from 100 percent down to 0 to choose from: master, background ambience, and decision loop.
Speech recognition turns spoken choices on or off.
Key bindings opens the key bindings screen.

Key bindings

Press F1 at any time, or choose Key bindings in Settings, to change the game's keys.
The screen opens on Back, and the actions follow it, each read with its keys.
Up and Down move through them.
Enter rebinds the action you are on: press the key you want, with Shift, Control or Alt if you like.
A adds a second key for it instead of replacing the one it has.
Delete unbinds it.
R twice resets every binding to the default.
Escape, F1 or Enter on Back goes back.
Escape, F1 and the number keys 1 to 9 can never be rebound.

Credits
