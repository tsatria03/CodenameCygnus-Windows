unrelease:

26.10.19-1:
The volume keys work during a preview too, as they do in the story.
Settings has four new rows: Volume step, how far each press of a volume key turns a volume, 5, 10, 20 or 25 percent; Volume speed, how often a held volume key repeats, from every 50 to every 500 milliseconds; Seek step, how far Left and Right skip, 5, 10, 15, 20, 30 or 60 seconds; and Seek speed, how often a held Left or Right repeats.
Left and Right now skip 10 seconds at first instead of 5; choose another seek step in Settings.
A volume's levels in Settings follow the volume step, so a step of 5 percent gives levels such as 35 and 95.
Holding a skip or volume key repeats faster, every 100 milliseconds instead of 150.
An episode's Start button is now called Start episode, as Restart episode and Replay episode are.
During a preview, Left and Right skip back or forward, and holding them keeps skipping; S skips the whole preview.
At the end of an episode, Preview next episode now comes before the next episode, which is now called Play next episode.
Making a choice plays the original's skip sound, and Enter and the number keys no longer say the choice again; a spoken choice is still said back, so you know which word was heard.

26.10.18-2:
Credits say that Reactive Studios has become Earplay and that the original app is no longer on the App Store, and credit theblindgamer50 for helping recover the episodes from version 1.3.2, the version this port plays.
While the game is listening for a spoken choice, the choice can only be spoken: Enter and the number keys say "Speak your choice.", and the arrows still read the choices. Once listening ends, the keys choose again.
While the game is listening for a spoken choice, V no longer turns speech recognition off, and says "Listening." instead, as the original's voice button did; once listening ends, V works again.
While you are in another window, the Mission 1 download no longer says its percentages, and plays only its sound.
The Mission 1 download plays a short push sound every 10 percent, as it says how far it has got.
Choosing Download Mission 1 plays the original's Store sound.
Agent profile in the main menu is now called Profile, as in the original.

26.10.18-1:
Mission 1's recordings come as a download of their own, about 469 megabytes, so the game's own download stays small: choose Download Mission 1 on an episode's screen, or Continue in the main menu, and the game fetches them, checks them once they arrive and keeps them for every later version.
Speech recognition: with it on, say a choice aloud, as in the original. The game says "Listening." after the first choice and listens for 15 seconds, or for a timed choice's whole seven, and V turns it on or off. Nothing it hears is recorded.
The Agent profile in the main menu says your dominant trait, the furthest episode you have reached, how many episodes you have completed, your four trait scales and which way each leans, and all 16 achievements, earned or not.
The game has 16 achievements of its own, kept on your computer in place of the original's online Game Center ones; each is announced the first time you earn it, at the next choice or at the end of the episode, never over the story.
Your progress is saved as you play, at every new part of the story, and Continue in the main menu takes you back to where you left off.
Each episode carries on the choices and traits of the one before it, as the original did, so Signum Cygni remembers what you did in every earlier episode.
Episodes lists all eight episodes and says whether each is in progress, completed or locked; each episode opens once you finish the one before it.
Each episode's own screen has its description and Start. One in progress has Continue and Restart episode, a finished one says what was said at its end and has Replay episode and a preview of the next, and a locked one's Start says why it cannot be played yet.
Restarting or replaying an episode asks first, with the original's warning, and Continue, Replay and Restart say what they are doing, such as "Continuing Art Show."
When an episode ends, a menu offers the next episode, a preview of it, and the episode's own credits, as the original's end of episode did.
A preview of the next episode can be played from its episode's screen and from the end of an episode, and Escape or P pauses it, with Resume, Restart and Return to menu.
The game opens as the original did, with a loading message and, the first time, its headphones notice, then the main menu, with the original's interface sounds.
Settings has the master, background ambience and decision loop volumes, each with its levels from 100 percent down to 0, a switch for speech recognition, and the key bindings.
Every menu has Back as its first row, and opens on it.
Credits lists Reactive Studios' team, as the original's Info screen did.
The choices come in the order the characters say them, such as BOLD before SECRETIVE in Delphi's briefing in Art Show, where the original could show them in any order.
The van escape in Signum Cygni ends the way your choice leads, GRAB IT or HANG ON, instead of either ending at random as in the original.
S skips straight on to the next choice across everything you have heard before, and says "Not heard yet" on a part you have not, so new story is never skipped by mistake.
Holding Left or Right skips on through the recording, and holding a volume key keeps turning it up or down.
Every episode can be played from start to end. After each question the first choice is read; the arrows, Home and End move through the choices, Enter or the number keys choose, and C reads them all again.
While the story plays, R plays the recording again, Left and Right skip 5 seconds back or forward, and H says where you are.
Escape or P pauses the story, with Resume, Restart episode, Settings and Return to menu.
F1 opens the key bindings from anywhere, where the episode keys can be changed; Escape, F1 and the number keys always stay as they are.
The game speaks through NVDA, through another screen reader, or through a Windows voice when no screen reader is running.
