Settings has the three volumes, each with its levels from 100 percent down to 0 to choose from, a switch for speech recognition, and the key bindings.
The game opens as the original did, with a loading message and, the first time, its headphones notice, then a main menu of Episodes, Settings, Credits and Quit, with the original's interface sounds.
Episodes lists all eight episodes, and each says its description before you start it.
Credits lists Reactive Studios' team, as the original's Info screen did.
The game has 16 achievements of its own, kept on your computer in place of the original's online Game Center ones; each is announced the first time you earn it, at the next choice or at the end of the episode, never over the story.
The choices now come in the order the characters say them, such as BOLD before SECRETIVE in Delphi's briefing in Art Show; 20 questions across the episodes had them the other way round.
S skips straight on to the next choice across everything you have heard before, and says "Not heard yet" on a part you have not, so new story is never skipped by mistake.
Holding Left or Right skips on through the recording, and holding a volume key keeps turning it up or down.
The pause menu can restart the episode from its beginning, after asking first.
Left, Right, Home and End also move through the choices, once the question has been asked.
Episodes can now be played from start to end: the choices are read after each question, and you choose with the arrows and Enter or the number keys, pause with Space, replay with R, skip 5 seconds with Left and Right, and hear where you are with H.
